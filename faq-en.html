Unusual noises (squeaking, humming, knocking, or whistling) indicate mechanical problems.

For example: loose parts, motor malfunction, or fan damage. In such cases, it's best to immediately turn off the system and contact a specialist to prevent serious damage.

Water leakage or dripping from the system most often indicates a malfunction in the drainage system.

The main cause is a clogged drain pipe — over time, dust, mold, and residue accumulate in the pipe, which blocks the flow of condensation (water) and causes it to back up into the building.

Other reasons might include: a damaged drain pump, a pipe installed with an incorrect slope, or a frozen indoor coil due to a dirty filter. Ignoring a water leak can damage walls, ceilings, and promote mold growth. Therefore, to resolve the problem and clear the drain pipes, it is necessary to call a technician promptly, as AC units frequently leak water due to drainage issues.

SEER and COP are the main indicators of your climate equipment's energy efficiency:

SEER (Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio): Measures the system's cooling efficiency during the summer season.
COP (Coefficient of Performance): Shows the heating efficiency in winter.

For example, if the unit's COP is 4, it means that for every 1 kW of electricity consumed, the system generates 4 kW of heat. Simply put: in residential or commercial spaces, the higher the SEER and COP indicators, the more powerful and energy-efficient the equipment is, which ultimately significantly reduces your utility bills.
